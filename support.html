A Solid Feed: Support

A Solid Feed opens YouTube's mobile website and hides what you switch off: Shorts, ads, posts and polls, Mixes, Playables, the videos under a video, and YouTube's Home feed. It marks sponsor reads on the progress bar and lets you skip them.

Something broken or missing?

Open an issue with what you saw and on which page.

Common questions

Something I hid shows up again. YouTube changed its page. Report it with the link above.

Where are my settings? Tap Settings, the scissors in the tab bar.

Is my data collected? No. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this made by YouTube? No. A Solid Feed isn't affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
