A Solid Feed: Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

A Solid Feed is an iPhone app that opens YouTube's mobile website and hides the kinds of content you switch off. We don't collect, store or share any data about you. There is no account with us, no analytics and no advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings (what to hide, what to skip), the time you've spent in the app and where you stopped each video (so it picks up there next time) are saved on your iPhone only. They never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

YouTube and Google

The app shows YouTube's own website. If you sign in, you sign in with Google directly, and YouTube and Google see what they see when you use YouTube in Safari, under Google's privacy policy. The app never sends your sign-in, your history or what you watch anywhere.

SponsorBlock

To mark sponsor reads and self-promotion in a video, the app asks SponsorBlock, a free crowdsourced service. It sends only the first 4 characters of a code made from the video's ID. About 60 marked videos share those characters, so SponsorBlock can't tell which one you're watching. Nothing about you or your YouTube account is sent. Like any website, SponsorBlock sees your IP address. You can switch this off in Settings.

Links to other sites

Links that leave YouTube open in Safari, outside the app.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact
